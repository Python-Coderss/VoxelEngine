# CHANGELOG

## Every Character Speaks, and Beds & Banners Are Real Multiblocks (Oct 17, 2026)

### The previously silent cast is voiced
Ancient builders, the player ("You"), the narrator and bosses used to perform
entirely in captions. They now speak their authored lines through a local
operating-system voice (Windows SAPI through PowerShell, with per-role rate and
pitch), so no model is bundled and no network service is called:

- `VoiceProfile` gives each role its own register — builders are measured and
  dry, the narrator is slower and lower, bosses sit below the builders, and the
  player answers brighter.
- `WindowsSapiTts` renders SSML to a mono WAV on a worker thread; lines are
  cached by profile+text, so a repeated line is synthesized once. Signed
  percentages travel as `n6`/`p6` codes because PowerShell's `-File` parser
  treats a leading minus as a parameter name.
- `CharacterVoice` publishes the exact authored text to the live captions,
  plays the rendered audio through `VillagerAudioManager.playRendered`, and
  falls back to the recorded clip corpus, then to caption-only, when no local
  voice exists. `-Dvoxel.voice.tts=off` forces that fallback.
- Cutscene beats (`CinematicSystem.publishScript`) are always voiced and never
  rate-limited; ordinary conversation keeps a 7 s per-speaker gap, and a line
  spoken both by a direct interaction and its cutscene is not doubled.
- Villagers keep their recorded add-on clips; `VoiceProfile.VILLAGER` never
  reaches the synthesizer.

### Headless and audioless verified
The whole suite (624 tests) passes with `-Djava.awt.headless=true` and
`ALSOFT_DRIVERS=null`. New coverage: `AudiolessVoiceTest` drives the bridge
with an uninitialized `VillagerAudioManager` (captions and cached renders still
happen, playback is a safe no-op), `DecorationModelsHeadlessTest` resolves every
decoration model's texture references straight from JSON with no GL context,
and `WindowsSapiTtsTest` pins the opt-out (`-Dvoxel.voice.tts=off`).

### Beds and standing banners join doors as multiblocks
- A bed is a foot cell plus a head cell on one horizontal axis. One `bed`
  item stamps both halves with the facing taken from the player's look,
  breaking either half removes the other, and every half drops one bed.
- A standing banner is a lower plus an upper cell with two cloth planes
  (north/south and east/west). It needs solid ground and a free cell above;
  breaking either half removes the column and drops one banner.
- `DecorBlocks` owns the id layout and partner math, `BedPair` and
  `BannerColumn` perform the voxel transitions, and `BlockInteraction` adds the
  player/entity/room checks. Models for four bed facings and both banner
  planes are generated by `tools/gen_bed_banner_models.py`.

## Doors Are Real Multiblocks Now (Oct 17, 2026)

A door item used to stamp a single lower block, so a placed door was half a
building block with nothing above it, and mining one half left the other
floating. Doors are now handled as one two-block column:

- **Placing** a door writes both halves at once. The wall axis comes from the
  face you click (side of a wall) or, when placing on a floor, from the
  direction you are looking, so the panel sits in the doorway instead of
  slicing across it.
- **Using** a door swings both halves to the 90°-rotated model and flips an
  open bit in the extra data. Using it again closes it. A column with a
  missing or mismatched half refuses to open rather than spawning a ghost
  panel.
- **Breaking** either half clears the other and drops exactly one door item;
  the block-to-item table already maps all four parts of each wood to one
  entry. Creative instant-break also clears the column.

The pair math lives in `DecorBlocks` (ids, partner, rotated counterpart) and
`DoorColumn` (place/toggle/remove against a `World`), both covered by
`DoorColumnTest` and `DecorationBlocksTest` without a GL context.

## Music Library, Guided Tutorial, Demo Worlds & a New Panorama (Oct 17, 2026)

### Every bundled soundtrack now feeds one music system
The game shipped three separate music piles — eight MCSM soundtrack albums under
`src/resources/music`, the Aether soundtrack under
`assets/aether/sounds/music`, and an empty `src/main/resources/music` folder the
old player scanned in vain. `MusicLibrary` now discovers all of them at boot,
filters placeholders, unused takes, sub-20-second stings and loop-layer scraps,
and classifies the survivors into fourteen pools: menu, calm, night, danger,
combat, boss, cave, nether, end, aether, sift, tutorial, village and story.
The shipped install resolves **257 playable tracks** (21 combat, 20 boss,
38 calm, 24 tutorial, 31 village, 5 Aether, and so on). Classification is a pure
function of the path, so it is unit tested.

`MusicDirector` gained `contextForScene(...)`: boss > combat > danger >
dimension mood > cave > night > tutorial/village > calm, with a legacy
`contextFor(...)` retained for the original four-pool tests. `MusicPlayer` now
decodes MP3 **and OGG** (Aether), does it on a daemon worker while the current
track keeps playing, and crossfades context changes instead of cutting. The old
inline decode froze the render thread for seconds on every track change.
`/music` reports the current pool/track and accepts `auto`, `off`, `on`, `next`,
`list` or a pool name.

### The tutorial is rebuilt and now teaches
- The bundled `tutorial_world` was **stale** — generated before the door
  overhaul, so every authored doorway was still a hole while the source had

  builds, spawn or usable starter kit.
- `Main.copyTutorialTemplate()` no longer depends on the working directory

  then rebuilds the world through `TutorialWorldExporter.exportTo(...)`.
- Six guided goals (`TutorialProgress`) track what the player actually does:
  mine, craft, smelt, forge iron gear, use a portal, loot the dungeon hall.
  A persistent top-left tracker shows the current step and hint; each completion
  fires a title card, letterbox beat and progress status. Actions are reported
  from the existing break/craft/furnace/portal/chest code paths.
- The starter kit now includes stone tools, torches, bread and food — the old
  one handed a survival player twenty Create blocks and nothing to dig with.

### Demo worlds

## Villager TV: eight channels with real scripts, cues and voices (Oct 6, 2026)

### The TV is now an actual station
Villager TV used to be four hard-coded text loops. It is now a broadcast: **eight
channels**, each with a callsign, a strapline, a tint and a running order of
named programmes — Off-Air, **VSN** (Villager Shopping Network), **WX** (Weather
& Time), **VNN** (Villager News), **VMC** (Villager Music Channel), **NAT**
(Villager Geographic), **KITCH** (Hrmm Kitchen) and **SPORT** (Villager Sports
Network). Channels 0-3 keep their original numbers, so saved worlds and `/tv`
still mean what they meant.

### The scripts are a document, not code
The whole schedule lives in `src/main/resources/voice/tv_guide.json`: channels →
programmes → scripted lines, each with an offset, a caption, the voice it is
spoken in and the music cue that starts with it. Rewriting a channel needs no
Java change. `TvGuide` parses it and rejects a broken schedule loudly (a line
outside its slot, an unknown cue, non-contiguous channel ids, a cue with no
notes) rather than broadcasting nonsense; a small built-in schedule is the
last-resort fallback so a packaging mistake can never leave a screen blank.
VNN alone scripts three programmes — the Nightly, a Nether field report and the
Cultural Corner — across 48 + 40 + 32 seconds.

### Music cues are sung, not sampled
Each cue is pure data (tempo, notes, gain) and renders through the **same**
clip-voice arrangement pipeline the Villager News intro already uses, so a theme
or a price sting is sung in villager syllables with no new audio stack and no
new binary assets. Twenty-two cues ship: `vnn_theme`, `headline_sting`,
`vsn_jingle`, `price_sting`, `weather_jingle`, `clock_chime`, `storm_warning`,
`vmc_theme`, `nature_bed`, `kitchen_jingle`, `sports_anthem`, `full_time` and
the rest. A cue's notes are fingerprinted into the render cache key, so editing
the score always produces fresh audio instead of a stale clip.

### Every voice is a real take from the consented collection
The station broadcasts from exactly one collection: the Villager News 1.0
Add-On recordings the index marks `kind="addon"`. `VillagerAudioManager` builds
an addon-only view of the catalog (`ClipIndex.restrictedToKind(TV_CLIP_KIND)`)
and resolves pinned clips, `topic` situation matches and caption matches all
through it, so a TV line can never be spoken by any other corpus. The scripts go
further and are *written from* that collection: all 87 lines are the transcripts
of actual takes, and `tools/bind_tv_clips.py` pins each line to the recording of
its own caption, so what the screen shows is what you hear. `--check` fails when
a pin is missing, names a non-addon clip, or says something other than the
caption. `Main` polls the system once per logical tick and only speaks on the
channel a viewer is watching, so a wall of screens left on the default channel
no longer narrates the whole village; tuning in plays the current line once and
then runs forward, and switching channels opens with a short station ident.

The Weather station keeps a **live strip** under its script
(`Live: Morning 6:40 AM | Clear skies, sunny`) so it still reports the actual
world clock and sky, which the old text-only channel did.

### On the requested specific voice actor
Nothing is cloned. The project already holds a collection of Villager News
recordings it is permitted to use, and the TV now draws exclusively from it —
the addon-only restriction above is enforced in code *and* asserted by
`TvAddonVoiceTest`, so a stray corpus clip cannot leak onto the station. A
different licensed or self-recorded pack still drops in cleanly through
`-Dvoxel.voice.index=<file>`; per-channel voice packs would additionally need
the audio manager to hold more than one clip library, which is an easy
follow-up.

Tests: `TvGuideTest` (8 — the shipped guide loads all eight channels, every
programme is well formed, every cue is renderable and referenced, every spoken
line names a real voice, slots loop, malformed guides are rejected, the
fallback is valid), `VillagerTVSystemTest` (14 — channel naming and clamping,
channel cycling and wrap, the display banner, tuning in, per-offset line firing,
programme rollover, silence while unwatched, cue on-air window, the live weather
strip, and the broadcast clock) and `TvAddonVoiceTest` (4 — the addon view is a
clean partition of the catalog that excludes every other collection, every
scripted line is pinned to an addon recording, every caption is a real take of
that line, and the station never leans on a single voice). Suite: **565 green**.

## Doors Everywhere & Structure Overhaul (Oct 6, 2026)

### Doors: the block that never existed
Door, trapdoor, ladder and flower-pot **textures and vanilla models shipped in
the resource tree, but no door block was ever registered** — so every house,
barn, keep and dungeon above was built with a bare air gap where its door should
be. Twenty decoration blocks now exist (`DecorBlocks`, IDs 950-969): four woods ×
lower/upper × both wall orientations, oak and iron trapdoors, a ladder and a
flower pot. `tools/gen_door_models.py` writes the 18 missing models (the `_ew`
variant is the vanilla panel rotated 90° about Y with its side faces remapped),
`Main.registerDecorativeBlocks()` registers every id with the block, shader and
model registries, and `ItemDefinitions.registerDoorItem` turns each wood into one
inventory item whose four block ids all map back to it, so breaking any half
returns the door you placed.

### Every authored structure rebuilt around the door
A door is only useful if the building around it is sound, so every authored
voxel structure and feature was reworked — with the deliberate exception of
trees:

- **Shared shape library** (`SiteShapes`, used by the whole MCSM / Legends /
  Dungeons / Ancient-Builder site families): the town now lays a curtain wall
  with a gated entrance plus eight real houses (floor, log corner posts,
  pyramid roof, lamp and a door facing the well) around a canopied well; the
  keep gained a gated gatehouse, a battlement course and an inner hall; the
  arena's four cardinal gates are real doors; the swamp hut grew walls, a gable
  roof, lit windows and a dock door instead of a floating roof over water; the
  sky-island shrine, the pumpkin barn, the rift hall and the forge are all now
  walled, roofed and gated, and the temple has a covered entrance passage with a
  double door. Roofs are stepped so their bottom course always rests on the wall
  ring; nothing floats.
- **Tutorial world**: the ASCII blueprint stamper understands two door
  characters (`D` for a wall along X, `V` for a wall along Z) and derives the
  upper half from the layer beneath, so a blueprint authors a door in exactly
  the way the halves are baked. Cottage, house, barn, chapel, windmill and
  watchtower blueprints gained doors — and three of them were missing entire
  walls. The castle and keep gates, the redstone lab, vault, smelting works,
  throne room, Villager News studio and the three Aether boss halls all traded
  three-wide wall-slits for two-wide doorways with the wall continuing above;
  the mineshaft entrance is walkable and the cinematic director's deck has a
  ladder.
- **Survival village**: small houses, large houses, the workshop and the
  Villager News studio front place oak, dark-oak and spruce doors in their
  doorways, and the farm's water source no longer lands outside its own plot.
- **Stronghold & fortress**: the tower rooms have working doors and a floor
  course (the corridor's middle lane had none), corner pillars reach their
  parapet, the lava is a contained basin rather than loose blocks, and the
  spawner stands on a plinth. The stronghold's library and stairwell were dead
  code — the library's bounding box never fitted a chunk and the stairwell was a
  solid stone cube with no stairs — and both now generate, with doors and a
  ladder.
- **Ancient Builder sites, the Ancient City and MCSM's hand-built locations**:
  every carved entrance is a real door. The temple's arch lantern, the
  graveyard's floating torches, the watch spire's scattered "spiral" stairs and
  the sealed quarry store were rebuilt; the Monument Garden basin holds its
  water; the glass tower has actual floor decks; the metro hall's girders rest on
  their platform; the research lab's shell no longer raises a wall down its own
  middle; and the pumpkin hideout has a ladder shaft in its corner instead of a
  shaft that punched through the boss pedestal.
- **Villager-built houses** place a real oak door, not a gap.
- A latent crash was fixed in the stronghold generator: `findBlockId` returns
  `null` for unknown names and the code unboxed it, asking for `stone_bricks` —
  a name this engine never registers (it is `stonebrick`/`stonebrick_normal`).

Tests: `DecorationBlocksTest` (3 — id table, twinned door halves, a model on
disk per name), `SiteShapesTest` grew door coverage and a check that no door

## Books & Restored Villagers (Oct 6, 2026)

### Books render with a real spine
Book models and 16x16 icons already sat in the resource tree, but no book was
registered as an item. Four books are now real items backed by miniature AABB
models instead of flat sprites: **Book**, **Book and Quill**, **Written Book**

Villages are not persisted, so a villager restored from a save came back with no

merges instead of overwriting an existing cell (a reloaded world re-registers
villages as its chunks generate), and `bindRestoredVillager` re-attaches a
restored villager to the village it stands in (or a rebuilt record), restores
membership and re-adopts a job site, so work and the barter panel resume.
`Main.restoreEntitiesForDimension` calls it for every restored villager.

## Villager Professions (Oct 5, 2026)

### Professions are real jobs now
- Every trade has a **job site** built from blocks villages already place: farms ->
  farmland/wheat, workshops -> crafting table, houses -> chests, the Villager News
  studio -> the TV block. A villager takes the trade of the nearest job site instead
  of rolling one at random, so a village's professions follow from what is actually
  standing in it.
- **Work loop:** `VillagerBrain` gained a `WORK` action in the utility order
  (panic > shelter > build > work > socialize > wander). Villagers walk to their
  job site and work it: farmers harvest ripe wheat (dropping grain through the
  world item sink) and plant bare farmland, anchors broadcast at the desk,
  traders tend the stall, builders keep hammering at the workbench between builds.
  Breaking a workbench sends its villager looking for another one.
- **Career ladder:** job XP (tend 2, plant 3, harvest 5, build 4, broadcast 3,
  trade 6) promotes villagers through five levels with their own titles
  (Novice Farmer -> Master Farmer, Junior Reporter -> Prime Anchor). Promotions
  are announced as a spoken line and every dialogue/status line now shows the
  career title instead of the bare profession.
- **Trading:** shift-right-click a villager with a trade to open the barter panel
  (career title, up to three offers, `1`-`3` to trade, `ESC` to close). Offers
  scale with career level: a Novice Trader sells coal and sticks, a Master
  Trader deals diamonds. Trades pay job XP, so a busy merchant can promote
  mid-session.
- **Villager spawning fixed:** the structure generator no longer spawns villagers
  during chunk generation — it queues the village, and `VillagerVillageManager`
  places the population once the ground under the village is loaded and the
  player is within 160 blocks. Villagers spawn on their real surface (never
  inside terrain, water, or an ungenerated column), get unique ids, and are
  added to the village population list.
- **Pressing V no longer makes nitwits:** fresh villagers get a weighted trade
  (farmer/builder/shopkeeper/anchor) and adopt the nearest job site; nitwits only
  exist when nothing around could employ them. Villages now generate farmland
  with crops and a chest per house so the new jobs have somewhere to happen.
- Tests: 390 total, 0 failures.

## Dumb-Human AI, Clip Voice, Live Captions (Oct 4, 2026)

### Comedic "dumb human" AI (villagers and mobs)
- New shared psyche layer `ComedyMind` with a per-entity personality (boldness,
  distractibility, drama, incompetence) derived from the entity id. Villagers
  and hostile mobs run on the same mind, so a hunter and a farmer make the
  same kinds of mistakes.
- **Short attention span**: villagers abandon tasks to investigate shiny
  things (`DISTRACTED`), forget what they were doing mid-task (`FORGOT`,
  "Wait. What was I doing?"), and stand admiring nothing (`ADMIRE`). Mobs
  break off chases because something else got interesting (`DISTRACT`).
- **Confident then cowardly**: villagers strut toward danger acting tough
  (`STRUT`, "I am not scared of you!") and scream the moment it crosses their
  boldness-based coward distance ("Okay I am a LITTLE scared of you!"). Bold
  hunters swagger in at walking speed taunting (`MOB_TAUNT`); timid ones cut
  and run at the first hit (`MOB_COWARD`) regardless of health.
- **Herd panic & contagion**: fleeing villagers follow whoever is ahead
  instead of choosing an escape direction (conga lines), fear adopted from
  neighbors amplifies with the chaos level, and hunters trail the nearest
  packmate mid-chase instead of flanking.
- **Job incompetence**: building is slapstick — proud "Nailed it." on
  success, "Ow! My thumb!" on failure, and forgetting the job entirely,

## Base Voice Overhaul — Numbers, Acronyms, Prosody (Aug 25, 2026)

### Base TTS frontend (`CoquiFrontend`)
- **Numbers are spoken, not dropped**: digit runs now expand to English words
  ("310" -> "three hundred and ten", "1,256" -> "one thousand...", "0" ->
  "zero", 4+ digit runs support thousands/millions/billions). Previously the
  digit symbols were silently removed from the token stream, so any line
  containing a number spoke only the words around it.
- **Contractions stay glued**: "what's"/"we're" keep the apostrophe inside the
  word and hit their CMU dictionary entry instead of falling back to
  letter-by-letter pronunciation.
- **All-caps acronyms are spelled** ("TV" -> "tee vee") using real CMU letter
  names (the weak article "a" is remapped to "ay" = /eɪ/).
- **Money and percent attach to numbers**: "$5"/"5$" -> "five dollars",
  "50%" -> "fifty percent"; decimals "3.5" -> "three point five".

### Base TTS synthesis (`CoquiVitsTts`)
- **Sentence joins use the validated 0.1 s gap** (2205 samples at 22.05 kHz)
  instead of Coqui's 450 ms pad — multi-sentence lines no longer plod.
- **Emotion-aware base synthesis**: the VITS noise-scale inputs now respond to
  the line's emotion (angry crisp, sad/scared breathier, happy brighter) so
  the *base* carries the delivery before RVC ever sees it.

### Other
- Voice cache version bumped to v8; old clips regenerate automatically.
- New frontend unit tests for numbers, contractions, acronyms, currency.

## Mob AI & Villager Voice Improvements (Aug 25, 2026)

### Mob AI
- **Enemies stop wall-hacking**: hostile mobs now only refresh their memory of
  the player's position with a clear line of sight (voxel raycaster). They still
  hunt the last seen spot for a few seconds, then lose interest instead of
  walking through walls to your exact position.
- **PathFinder relaxation fix**: A* now keeps the best-known cost per cell and
  re-opens cells only when a strictly cheaper route is found, so a worse
  predecessor can never overwrite the came-from chain (previously produced
  bloated routes on flat terrain and wasted expansions).
- **Villager panic flees around obstacles**: on spotting a threat, villagers
  immediately pathfind an escape point opposite the monster (jittered so two
  villagers don't queue), fall back to the zigzag sprint when no route exists,
  and re-path when pinned against a wall.
- **Villagers pathfind home at night** instead of grinding into walls, and
  **wanderers now detect being stuck** and pick a fresh target instead of
  bumping an obstacle for the full 14 s window.
- New tests: raycaster line of sight, optimal-cost pathfinding, flee-point
  geometry; existing AI/voice suites updated and green.

### RVC voice
- **Per-line noise seed**: each dialogue line now draws its own deterministic
  RVC posterior noise (seed hashed from the clip), so artifacts no longer
  repeat identically on every line while the persistent voice cache stays valid.
- **Energy-scaled excitation**: the RVC noise input is scaled per frame by local
  RMS energy — pauses and unvoiced consonants carry less random excitation,
  cutting the steady hiss RVC synthesized into the gaps between words.
- **Median-smoothed pitch contour**: single-frame autocorrelation outliers
  (which RVC rendered as chirps/crackles) are removed with a 3-tap median over
  voiced runs only; real rises and falls are untouched.
- **Click-free output padding**: if the RVC graph returns a few samples short,
  the tail is now fade-ramped instead of hard-zeroed (no truncation click).
